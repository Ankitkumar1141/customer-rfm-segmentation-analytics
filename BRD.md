# Business Requirements Document (BRD)
**Project:** Customer RFM Segmentation Analytics  
**Author:** Ankit Kumar  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** October 2026

---

## 1. Project Purpose

The business wants to understand which customers are most valuable and which are at risk of churning. Leadership currently has no structured view of customer value, so retention efforts are reactive rather than targeted.

---

## 2. Scope

- **In scope:** Customer segmentation using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis, churn identification, and an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- **Out of scope:** Real-time data pipelines, predictive ML models, customer-level outreach automation.

---

## 3. Business Questions

| # | Question | Priority |
|---|----------|----------|
| 1 | Who are our most valuable customers, and how much revenue do they contribute? | High |
| 2 | Which customers have gone quiet and are at risk of churning? | High |
| 3 | How does customer value trend across time (monthly, quarterly, yearly)? | Medium |
| 4 | How does customer value and churn differ across geographic markets? | Medium |
| 5 | What specific actions should the business take per segment? | High |

---

## 4. Key Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Recency** | Days since a customer's most recent purchase |
| **Frequency** | Number of distinct orders placed by a customer |
| **Monetary** | Total revenue generated by a customer |
| **RFM Score** | Composite 1–5 score per dimension; higher = more valuable |
| **Churned** | Customer with no purchase in the last 90 days |
| **Champion** | Customer with top scores across all three RFM dimensions |

---

## 5. Success Criteria

- All 5 business questions must be answerable directly from the Power BI dashboard.
- Dashboard must support filtering by time (month/quarter/year), country, segment, and churn status.
- Row count at each SQL cleaning step must be documented and validated against UCI source totals.
